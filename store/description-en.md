# Screw Puzzle — Store Submission Materials

## Short description (CrazyGames / GameDistribution / itch.io)

Unscrew colorful plates stacked in layers and sort the screws in the tray.
Covered screws won't budge — clear the plates above first. Match 3 screws of
a color to free the tray. 60 levels of satisfying metal-clicking puzzles.

## Details

- Genre: Puzzle / Casual / Sorting
- Controls: Mouse / Touch (tap a screw to unscrew it). Keyboard: R restart, Esc menu.
- Features: Layered cover mechanic (a screw pinned by a plate above must wait),
  8-slot tray with match-3 clearing, +1 slot power-up once per level, 60
  progressive levels (3→10 plates, 3→7 colors, 2→4 layers), star rating by
  time, progress saving, procedural graphics & synthesized sounds, single-file
  HTML5 (no external assets), works offline once loaded.
- Tech: Vanilla JavaScript, Canvas, WebAudio. No frameworks, no tracking.
